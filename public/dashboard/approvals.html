Persistent Mac app access

Checking permission…

When enabled, Codex can access any installed app without asking for each app again, including after OmniX Go restarts. This does not authorize sensitive actions such as sending messages, purchases or deletion, and does not override macOS privacy permissions.

Each request expires after 45 seconds. “Remember this app” applies only to the exact approved app within this OmniX Go service session, and never grants blanket approval to other actions.
